## Implemented

Full and Custom reuse the pinned source compiler, admission, candidate
validation, bounded structural repair, deterministic enrichment and atomic
accepted-result pipeline. Full uses the exact bundle Wire 1.0; Custom retains
only its selected arrays and the mandatory structural entity/source namespace.
Semantic profile and provider/runtime profile remain independent.

Usage, recommendations and uncertainties map to canonical 1.2 and the same
immutable Ledger rows. Usage requires actual-use stages; MENTIONED is rejected.
Kind-specific nullable fields, attribution, qualifiers and source anchors are
preserved. Full/Custom observation identity includes candidate hash, module and
position; the legacy Economy identity and canonical shape remain unchanged.

Contradictions reference two distinct existing selected typed observations in
the same candidate. Foreign, missing, self, identical and cyclic endpoints are
rejected. Relations retain both sides' evidence/entity/qualifier unions and
SOURCE_INFERENCE/UNCLEAR attribution. Ledger verifies the projection before
any effects. Empty selected modules can retain an independently verified
internal source pin without fabricating Evidence or adding a canonical field.

Chunk outputs are remapped in source order, including typed relation endpoints,
regardless of delivery order. Relations across chunks are not synthesized.
Selected contradiction scope on a multi-chunk result explicitly reports
WITHIN_CHUNKS_ONLY and coverage_complete=false; Daily shows partial readiness.
No additional model request is implied. GUI labels a possible contradiction
as an analysis interpretation, links both existing statements and exposes
each side's source Evidence. Structural-only entities are labelled explicitly.

Primary Full/Custom assignments are supported. Translation/history/extra-pass
options remain unavailable until their handlers exist (CP-6.8). Actual admission
still requires task capability, current free eligibility, quality scope and
fresh quota; saving or assigning a semantic profile grants none of these.
