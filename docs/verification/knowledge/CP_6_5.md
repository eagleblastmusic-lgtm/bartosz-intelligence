## Historical failures and fixes

   Split source views into separately bounded groups; identical regression passed.
2. Compound source ordering repeatedly sorted all remaining documents; future
   JSON summaries were also scanned while indexing titles. Cursor indexes plus
   sequential disjoint source ranges eliminate both repeated scans.
3. Initial large-corpus query timed out while sorting the entire hit set.
   Native FTS rowid keyset ordering fixed the query; the unchanged 120 s corpus
   test passes. A short signing-secret fixture and a variable-renaming typo were
   local test/implementation failures and were corrected without changing data
   or lowering acceptance.

   bounded retest passed. Preserve `CP_6_5_GATE_S_LOCAL_FAIL.json` alongside final
   `CP_6_5_GATE_S_LOCAL.json`.
