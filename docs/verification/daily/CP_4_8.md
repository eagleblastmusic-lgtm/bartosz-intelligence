The report pins built asset SHA-256, network/device/cache conditions, individual trials, 24 page/width/theme axe audits (Daily, Channels, System), calendar/focus/touch checks and unchanged actual backend counters. All 24 axe/WCAG A/AA page audits and calendar audit have no violations. Reading and Evidence drawer axe checks also PASS in the transport/attribution E2E. Automated focus restore, original quotation handling, 4×2 reflow, reduced motion, token contrast and 200% equivalent effective viewport checks pass. It has explicit publication uncertainty and does not place unknown dates in Today. Targeted test verifies three bounded GETs and no mutations.

Nine affected GUI regressions PASS: owner/session clearing, onboarding/idempotency, Daily filters/races/calendar and undated, canonical verified reading, Evidence/focus/source links, and System unknown/freshness semantics. Workspace and Worker-test typecheck, lint, affected formatting, web build and diff checks PASS. A read-only synthetic manual-review mode is available with `--serve-review`; only loopback GETs are exposed, and no real owner credentials or AI calls are involved. Its synthetic previews intentionally are not full source-backed detail records and cannot be used as a live vertical-slice proof. No final Stage handoff is created before acceptance.
